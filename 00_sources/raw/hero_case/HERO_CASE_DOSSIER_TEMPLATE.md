## Before you start — three rules

1. **Sanitise, don't simplify.** Change names, exact figures and dates freely. Do **not** flatten the structure: if the real case had a four-layer ownership chain with a nominee shareholder, keep four layers and keep the nominee. Structural complexity is the entire point — it is what the prototype has to prove it can handle.
2. **"Unknown" is a valid answer.** Write `UNKNOWN` and, where you can, `who would know`. An honest gap gets designed as a visible unknown state. A guess gets designed as a fact, and then gets contradicted in the workshop.
3. **One case, filled deeply, beats three cases filled thinly.** If you only have time for one, do Case 1 and stop.

Fill this in prose — bullet fragments are fine. Do not worry about consistency of format; I will normalise it.
