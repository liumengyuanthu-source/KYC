4. The existing roles merge Risk / Financial Crime / QA and Legal / Credit / Conflicts. The PPT distinguishes QA, Financial Crime / Compliance, Control Room, Legal and Credit responsibilities.
5. Existing `03_personas_journey/pain_point_register.csv` contains five S2 design hypotheses; it is not a complete transcription or role mapping of source slides 4–5.

## Proposed design, not implemented

Recommended: source process axis + distinct role lanes + role-specific views of shared scenarios. Retain S1–S5 as a summary navigation layer, not as replacement numbering. Show business titles first and process references in restrained secondary text.

Each relevant role activity opens an explicit scenario view: role goal → Current activity and pain → Target change → input / output and handoff → existing product action. Inapplicable cells say there is no direct task; do not invent activities just to fill the matrix. Role selection changes the narrative, not authorization.

Keep the same synthetic case, stable scenario IDs and approved business engines. Current / Target, language, scroll position and return context must persist. Presentation controls remain read-only. Source ambiguities use explicit annotations; unavailable actions explain why and provide a safe return.

Alternatives considered: separate journeys per role (clear individually, but duplicated handoffs and weaker shared-case continuity); retain only the five-stage layout with more cards (smaller change, but insufficient process-number fidelity).

## Required verification after approval

- Source coverage: all numbered source steps and slide 4–5 pains accounted for as mapped, ambiguous or adjacent scope.
- Role coverage: each source role has meaningful scenario entry points; no invented full role × stage coverage.
- Interaction: role change, scene open / close, pointer drag vs click, touch, keyboard and focus restoration.
- Continuity: Current → Target → Prototype → original scene / journey, language switch and full-content print.
- Business safety: role view, Play / Next and media cannot create approvals or clear the case; existing A–F regression remains intact.
