## Existing business boundaries retained

- Finding remains `DEMO-CTT-001/finding/person-t-c01` when present; older Entity A findings cannot substitute.
- Person T's employment by B and coordination claim for A do not establish authority.
- `request_identity` uses the existing C-to-B request contract. New identity purpose does not inherit coordination evidence sufficiency or broaden prior grants.
- The original request's review/dispatch/delivery states remain separate; received information remains unassessed until a valid purpose assessment.
- Query inputs, provider snapshots and comparison versions remain bound to the original result. Unknown or year-only birth information cannot become a proven difference.
- Only eligible existing commands may create a draft, request or local human outcome. No new approval/release/publish/trade command is introduced.
- Credit / Legal / Conflicts / EDD / QA remain independent conditions. A completed local action is not a whole-case result.

## Deliberately distinct contexts
