# Distribution and Confidentiality Rules

## Approved labels

- Confidential Australian Banking Client
- Strategic APAC Banking Account
- Institutional Clear-to-Trade Product
- Agentic AI KYC Clear-to-Trade Product

## Prohibited content

- guessed or implied client identity;
- unapproved logos or branded references;
- real personal data in synthetic scenarios;
- credentials, access tokens or OAuth data;
- unconfirmed volumes, staffing, KPIs, SLAs or system ownership;
- claims of autonomous KYC, QA or Clear-to-Trade approval;
- final architecture, vendor or technology decisions before approval.

All shared outputs must be scanned using the Christina-approved prohibited-term configuration. A missing approved configuration blocks the confidentiality gate.
