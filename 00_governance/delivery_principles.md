# Delivery Principles

1. Work hypothesis-led without presenting hypotheses as confirmed facts.
2. Derive product direction from the role-based journey.
3. Maintain one traceable content spine across every deliverable.
4. Preserve M0–M8 plus Legal C1 and Credit C2 references.
5. Use one common journey with trigger-specific deltas.
6. Separate MVP Human-in-the-Loop from target Human Review by Exception.
7. Define capabilities before platforms and product behaviour before architecture.
8. Keep accountable human roles, stop conditions, overrides and audit records explicit.
9. Use stable IDs and controlled change impact.
10. Keep the repository as source of truth and Miro as an editable visual view.
