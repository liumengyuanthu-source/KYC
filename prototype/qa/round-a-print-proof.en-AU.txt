01 · SCN-REQUIREMENTS · S2 · M2.1–M2.5

Make requirements explicit
Current
Determine AML and non-AML requirements, exceptions and risk indicators for the relationship.

Target
Prepare a versioned requirement set. Show the subject, purpose and basis of each requirement; retain unknown
applicability.

Wait / input: Scope and policy context must be known. Exceptions require a designated reviewer.
Human boundary: Confirm the demo requirement set. No client policy is inferred.
Expected result: Requirements confirmed for this synthetic scope; evidence still needs assessment.

02 · SCN-SOURCE · S2 · M3.1–M3.2

Use evidence already available
Current
Search public and commercial sources, then identify residual gaps. This already exists in the Current map.

Target
Link a source item to a specific subject, requirement and purpose, with its original version intact.

Wait / input: Access permission, source provenance and purpose-specific sufficiency.
Human boundary: Review proposed reuse. One accepted file does not satisfy every requirement.
Expected result: Entity identity may be supported; screening disambiguation remains a separate use.
SRC-007 · M3.1–M3.2 / SRC-010 §6 · Design hypothesis + synthetic record · J-M3-01




03 · SCN-GAP · S2 · M2.5 · M3.2–M3.3

Ask only for remaining gaps
Current
Issue requirements and request outstanding information from the client.

Target
Consolidate only unresolved facts into a purpose-specific request, with an owner and response link.

Wait / input: A permitted sender confirms the request; the review then waits for the named response.
Human boundary: Review the missing fact, purpose and response owner before simulating release.
Expected result: Request recorded locally. No email is sent. Receipt returns evidence to review.
SRC-007 · M2.5 · M3.2–M3.3 / SRC-010 §6 · Design hypothesis + synthetic record · J-M3-02, J-M3-03
04 · SCN-VALIDATE · S2 · M3.4–M3.5

Assess evidence for its purpose
Current
Capture and validate evidence; maintain outstanding gaps and the sourcing trail.

Target
Keep the artifact and its use assessment separate. A received supplement needs its own reviewed assessment.

Wait / input: The received supplement and a reasoned sufficiency assessment for the screening purpose.
Human boundary: A configured demo reviewer records sufficiency with rationale; bank authority remains open.
Expected result: Evidence use assessed. Screening still requires a separate human disposition.
SRC-007 · M3.4–M3.5 / SRC-010 §6 · Design hypothesis + synthetic record · J-M3-04




05 · SCN-MATCH · S3 · M4.5–M4.7

Resolve a possible match
Current
Adjudicate possible hits, assess materiality and record the screening outcome.

Target
Prepare subject-to-record comparisons and evidence-use links. A human records the disposition or requests more
information.

Wait / input: Sufficient current evidence, rationale and explicit simulated authority. Similarity is not a decision.
Human boundary: Only the demo reviewer may record disposition. No autonomous approval or automatic EDD.
Expected result: This finding is resolved for its input version. Legal, Credit and final QA remain open.
SRC-007 · M4.5–M4.7 / SRC-010 §6 · Design hypothesis + synthetic record · SCN-MATCH
06 · SCN-READINESS · S5 · M8.1

Know exactly what remains
Current
Confirm completion of all applicable clearance prerequisites.

Target
Derive a versioned snapshot from explicit conditions, each with its own owner, reason and next action.

Wait / input: Legal execution, Credit conditions, QA review and any unresolved applicability.
Human boundary: Final bank authority is not configured. Publishing and real trades are outside this slice.
Expected result: Not ready is an explainable result, not a percentage. Authorisation and publication stay separate.
