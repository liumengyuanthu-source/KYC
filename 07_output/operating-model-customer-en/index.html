Controls and human handoff

Shared by all actions; outputs and additional restrictions are action-specific above. All writes require existing business commands. This static package performs none.

Preconditions

  • Input objects exist with explicit source and version
  • The current actor or tool has the required read and action scope
  • No applicable prohibiting hold exists
  • Do not execute automatically if required policy or accountability is unknown

Stop

Pause the affected work if population is incomplete, a source fails, identity information is insufficient, permission is missing or materiality awaits judgement. Keep this action pending when its required inputs or permissions are absent.

Resume

After new evidence, query results or an authorised decision arrive, reassess only affected items. Recheck read/write permissions, current versions and the original task / return anchor. Opening a task or pressing Next never authorises resumption.

Shared forbidden effects

  • Create or alter bank policy or permissions
  • Overwrite historical evidence or decisions
  • Change business region, lists or applicability on a language switch
  • Record whole-case clearance without a valid basis
  • Exclude identity or declare global absence of risk from similarity or no results

Skills & Reuse

Every use independently requires current subject / purpose scope, permissions and versions. Shared families do not share prompts, access grants or professional standards. The authorised human consumer retains identity, materiality, sufficiency, authority and clearance decisions.
