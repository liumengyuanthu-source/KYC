# Batch D local review route

Mainline: http://127.0.0.1:8765/prototype/?scene=SCN-CREDIT&locale=en-AU

This is the existing local prototype, not a published service. It uses synthetic case DEMO-CTT-001 and approved demonstration source SRC-019. Opening the URL or playing a diagram does not load the D fixture or make a business decision.

1. Open Credit or Conflicts in the central scenario modal. Compare Current and Target. D1–D5 describe parallel work, not mandatory serial prerequisites.
2. Use **Archive session & load approved D entry** to explicitly enter the authored continuation. The pre-D session is retained locally.
3. Open **Condition Detail**. Switch Conflicts / Credit / Legal. Credit condition approval, Legal internal approval, agreement execution, authority and overall readiness are separate.
4. From Legal open **View dependency · DG-D02**. Inspect AI-01 and AGR-01 revision 03. Focus, Next, Play and References are read-only. Return to the same condition and then the original Current scenario.
5. In the facilitator role, enter a simulation rationale and explicitly revise the Credit input. The new input revision is appended; AGR-01 revision 03 still consumes the earlier revision. Check the stale-input notice and immutable history.
6. Switch to RM or client to inspect the safe progress view. These UI projections do not constitute production access control; no real restricted bank data is present.
7. Inspect D5 Readiness. Conflicts, Legal execution, screening coverage and QA remain unresolved; the case is **Not Ready**.
8. Restore the archived pre-D session to resume A–C actions. Their writers are deliberately disabled while D is active because their earlier recalculation logic does not preserve D dependencies.

No Batch E behavior, production integrations, bank permissions, monetary limits or SLAs are supplied by this increment. Media is static Archify plus host presentation controls; no new video is required. Black/blue/white warm-glass styling is retained; background blur/highlights are simulations, not optical refraction.

Evidence and completion status are in `2026-09-07-d2-batch-d-review.md` and `../00_governance/batch-d/red-team-results.md` when generated. Follow their actual passed / failed / not-run results rather than treating this route as test evidence.
